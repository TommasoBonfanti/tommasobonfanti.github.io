Optical trapping behind a transparent membrane

Research project, Photonics Lab, ETH Zurich · Feb. 2026 – Jun. 2026

Authors
Tommaso Bonfanti
Supervisors
Prof. Lukas Novotny

Summary

Simulated the vectorial focal field through a stratified medium to predict how an inserted glass slab broadens the focus and weakens the trap, and measured trap frequencies to test the model.

Designed a UHV-compatible assembly in SolidWorks to hold a graphene membrane between the focusing lens and the trapped particle, and built the experiment from an empty optical table: assembled and pumped down a new vacuum chamber, aligned the trapping path and forward QPD detection.

The full report is available upon reasonable request. Contact me.
