Tunable microlens arrays for neutral-atom readout

QuanTech project, ETH Zurich & PSI · Sep. 2026 – Ongoing

Summary

Four-month project at the Nanophotonics Systems Laboratory (ETH) and the Experimental Quantum Engineering group (ETH–PSI), on tunable microlens arrays for fast readout in neutral-atom quantum processors. Team of four, selected by commission review of a self-written proposal.

Currently optimizing the thermo-optic microlens array design for integration into the EQE neutral-atom setup.

The full report is available upon reasonable request. Contact me.
